Benefit Agency program

Dividend

Dividend is a monthly payment to New Jersey residents who apply. Everyone receives $100 a month, and people earning less receive more.

Qualification requirements

You qualify if all of these are true:

  1. 1You have a valid identity credential in your digital wallet.Issued by your state, and not changed since.
  2. 2You live in New Jersey.Your identity credential shows your state.
  3. 3Any income. Your monthly income sets your payment.$100 a month for everyone, plus up to $300 more below $1,200 a month, as of September 2026.

Monthly income is one month of gross pay, before tax, from the income credentials in your wallet.

How your payment is worked out

Everyone receives
$100
a month
Plus up to
$300
a month more, reduced by 25¢ for each dollar you earn
The extra ends at
$1,200
a month of income

Earning more never leaves you worse off

Below $1,200 a month, each extra dollar you earn lowers your Dividend by 25¢, so you keep 75¢. Above $1,200 you keep the whole dollar. The money in hand goes up at every step.
