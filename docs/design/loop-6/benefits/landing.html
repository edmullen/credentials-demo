Benefit Agency

Programs to support your needs

Benefit Agency runs five programs for New Jersey residents. You apply for all five at once with your digital wallet, so there's no form to fill in and no pay stubs to upload.

Our programs

  • Food Assistance Help buying groceries each month.
  • Energy Assistance Help paying heating, cooling and electricity bills.
  • Housing Assistance Help with rent, with a limit set by your county.
  • Health A discount on the Public Option health plan.
  • Dividend A monthly payment of at least $100.
This is a demo. For more info, view this repo.
