Benefit Agency program

Housing Assistance

Housing Assistance helps with the cost of rent. Housing costs differ across New Jersey, so the income limit depends on the county you live in.

Qualification requirements

You qualify if all of these are true:

  1. 1You have a valid identity credential in your digital wallet.Issued by your state, and not changed since.
  2. 2You live in New Jersey.Your identity credential shows your state.
  3. 3Your annual income is at or below the limit for your county.30% of your county’s area median income, as of September 2026.

Income means gross pay, before tax, from the income credentials in your wallet. Annual income is one month’s total × 12.
